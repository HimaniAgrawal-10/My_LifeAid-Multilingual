Emergency SOS

In an emergency, stay calm. Choose the service you need and tap the call button.

If there is immediate danger or you are unsure which service to contact, call 112 — India's unified emergency number.

Call 112 Now

Important: LifeAid does not dispatch an ambulance or automatically notify authorities. These buttons open your phone dialler; confirm the call. Emergency number availability can vary by state and service.

Personal Emergency Contacts

Keep trusted contacts ready

Save family members, a trusted friend or caregiver in your phone contacts. LifeAid currently provides this guidance; it does not store or automatically call personal contacts.

Call emergency service 112
When calling for help:
  • Clearly tell the operator what happened and where help is needed.
  • Share a nearby landmark and your callback number.
  • Follow the operator's instructions and do not hang up until advised.
  • Do not delay an emergency call while searching this website.
